# WeSyndic Apps 🏢

WeSyndic Apps is the frontend application of a modern **Property Management SaaS platform** designed for syndics, residents, and property managers.

It centralizes all aspects of condominium management into a single intelligent system: residences, buildings, units, finances, maintenance, communication, and AI-powered insights.

---

## 🚀 Overview

WeSyndic transforms traditional property management into a **digital SaaS ecosystem** with a focus on:

- Smart multi-residence management
- Financial operations automation
- Maintenance & incident tracking
- Resident communication system
- AI assistance integration
- Smart building ScoreCard analytics

---

## 🧠 Key Features

- 🏢 Multi-tenant architecture (SaaS ready)
- 👥 Resident & syndic management
- 💰 Finance (charges, payments, budgets, ledger)
- 🔧 Maintenance & incidents tracking
- 💬 Messaging & notifications
- 📊 Smart ScoreCard (building rating system A+ → D)
- 🤖 AI integration
- 🌍 Multi-language support (FR / AR RTL)
- 🌙 Dark / Light mode

---

## 🎯 Vision

WeSyndic aims to modernize the property management industry by providing a **scalable SaaS platform** that improves transparency, efficiency, and collaboration between syndics and residents.
